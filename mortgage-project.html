Mortgage Prediction

Graduate consulting-style project with Deluxe focused on estimating the probability that a household would take out a mortgage.

Overview

This project involved preparing large financial and demographic datasets, engineering features, building classification models, and presenting insights to stakeholders.

Methods

  • Data cleaning and preparation
  • Feature engineering across credit, income, and demographic indicators
  • Classification modeling
  • Accuracy and ROC-based evaluation

Outcome

The work simulated a real-world consulting engagement and focused on customer acquisition targeting in financial services.
